Salsa Sync v2.21

How it works

Prop your phone or laptop 2.5–3 m away, low down and tilted up, with your whole body (head to feet) in frame and the volume up. Tap Start camera once. After that you don't need to touch it: raise both hands above your head for 2 seconds to open the menu, then hold a hand over a button until it fills up. Raising both hands during a session or step check stops it.

A session and step check both start with a body check: raise and lower your hands, then stand still for 3 seconds. A session then counts you in with "5, 6, 7, 8" blips and you dance two practice basics, starting on the green 1. A second, higher "5, 6, 7, 8" means the real thing starts on the next 1, and that part is recorded. Step check flashes L or R each time it sees a foot land.

Bluetooth headphones and speakers play the music late, which makes every step look late. Run Audio sync in Settings (or from the menu) once for each pair of headphones or speakers: march on the spot to 16 clicks and it measures the delay. Filming from slightly to one side helps when your feet cross in front of each other.

Settings

Audio sync for your headphones or speakers

Bluetooth headphones and speakers play the music late, which makes every step look late. Do this once for each pair of headphones or speakers: march on the spot, one step on every click, and it measures the delay.

Step sensitivity for your camera and room

Before every session and step check, a 3 second body check (stand still) measures how much the camera's points wobble and sets how high a foot must lift to count as a step.
