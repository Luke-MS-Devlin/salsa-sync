Salsa Sync v2.15

How it works

Prop your phone or laptop 2.5–3 m away, low down and tilted up, with your whole body (head to feet) in frame and the volume up. Tap Start camera once. After that you don't need to touch it: raise both hands above your head for 2 seconds to open the menu, then hold a hand over a button until it fills up. Raising both hands during a session or step check stops it.

A session and step check both start by asking you to raise and lower your hands, then stand still for 3 seconds. A session then plays a "3, 2, 1, Go!" countdown and two practice basics. A second, higher "3, 2, 1, Go!" means the real thing starts on the next 1, and that part is recorded. Step check flashes L or R each time it sees a foot land.

Bluetooth headphones and speakers play the music late, which makes every step look late. Press Sync once per device: march on the spot, one step on every click, and it sets the audio delay for you. Filming from slightly to one side helps when your feet cross in front of each other.
